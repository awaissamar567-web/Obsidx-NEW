# Obsidx launch checklist

1. Set `OBSIDX_APPLICATION_WEBHOOK_URL` in Vercel to a private Make, Zapier, Airtable, or Supabase intake webhook.
2. Send a test application and confirm that the record and notification arrive.
3. Add approved client screenshots and videos to the Results section when they are available.
4. Add only verified review totals, names, revenue figures, and audience metrics.
5. Connect the chosen analytics provider to Apply Now clicks and successful form submissions.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with Tailwind CSS, deployed to Vercel. Static-first landing site with a separate application route and a thin serverless submission layer.

## Users

Creators and creator-led business owners who already have an audience and audience trust, but do not yet have a dependable monetization system behind their content.

## Product Purpose

Obsidx operates behind creators to turn attention and expertise into owned revenue systems. The website exists to qualify interested creators and convert them into applications.

## Positioning

Obsidx is an accountable growth partner for creators. It designs and runs products, memberships, SaaS concepts, conversion journeys, and recurring-revenue systems through clear strategy, visible numbers, and shared decisions.

## Operating Context

The visitor arrives from creator-led social content or referrals, checks proof, learns how the behind-the-scenes operating model works, and decides whether to apply for a limited engagement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One long-scroll landing page with Home, Results, and About Us anchors plus a separate `/apply` route.
- The main action is an Apply Now submission.
- No dashboard, login, multi-page navigation, or general agency portfolio.
- The application uses a four-step flow and a serverless webhook route. Production submission requires `OBSIDX_APPLICATION_WEBHOOK_URL`.
- Performance target is a sub-2-second perceived load with lazy-loaded proof media.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Obsidx.
- Voice: direct, selective, calm, commercially literate, and creator-friendly.
- Core line: “Built With Clarity. Designed To Compound.”
- Dark-first identity with gold as the sole warm accent.
- The visual reference is Nexus Creator's proof-led structure, adapted into a distinct Obsidx system.

## Product Principles

1. Proof earns the application.
2. One page, one action.
3. Explain the invisible-operator model in concrete language.
4. Selectivity should feel focused, not hostile.
5. Never publish claims that cannot be supported.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus, label every field, and provide reduced-motion and reduced-transparency fallbacks.

# Obsidx Design System

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

Obsidx is a clear operating system for creator businesses. The experience emphasizes visible decisions, shared accountability, and measurable commercial progress. It uses proof density where evidence matters and long quiet passages where the positioning needs room.

Mode: Persuade.

Design dials: variance 7, motion 6, density 4.

## Color

- Primary background: `#0F0F0F`
- Deep background: `#0A0A0A`
- Elevated surface: `#151412`
- Primary text: `#F7F4EE`
- Secondary text: `#AAA6A0`
- Gold accent: `#FCBA4B`
- Gold highlight: `#FFD58E`

Gold is the only accent family. It marks action, active signal, and the operating side of the business.

## Typography

- Hero display: Cormorant Garamond semibold italic for an Apple editorial feel.
- Section headings: Open Sauce Sans bold italic.
- Body and interface: Inter.
- Pull quotes only: Cormorant Garamond italic.
- Display tracking never exceeds `-0.04em`; body copy remains near neutral tracking.

## Shape and Material

- Cards and large surfaces: 16px radius.
- Fields: 10px radius.
- Buttons and compact filters: pill radius.
- Glass is reserved for floating navigation, the hero operator caption, and the application form.
- Materials use inner highlights and offset shadows. No glow-only elevation.

## Layout

- Page max width: 1440px.
- Three anchored chapters on the landing page: Home, Results, and About Us, followed by a focused application CTA.
- The application lives on `/apply` as a four-step qualification flow.
- The hero is centered and typographic. Mobile collapses to one column below 768px.
- Results is the densest chapter. About Us alternates a public-side/backstage comparison with sparse operating principles.

## Motion

- Lenis provides the same eased wheel-scroll foundation observed on Consulting.com.
- One orchestrated hero arrival establishes hierarchy.
- Scroll-linked reveals are limited to proof cards, operating principles, and the pull quote.
- Buttons respond on press and settle without bounce.
- Reduced motion removes transforms and keeps content visible.

## Content Rules

- One CTA label everywhere: Apply Now.
- Navigation uses an expandable vector dock with accessible labels and tooltips.
- Applications ask only for name, email, platform, and current monthly revenue.
- Do not publish client names, reviews, revenue figures, follower counts, or outcome screenshots without verification.
- Selectivity should read as focus and care, not status theater.
- The copy speaks in concrete business terms: offers, products, sales journeys, operations, and revenue systems.
